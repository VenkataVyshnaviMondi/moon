# Mooncrafters agency home

Mode: Persuade. User-directed redesign, built in code. The user pins navy, bright accents, restrained gradients, bold typography, a lightweight hero, grouped services and fast loading. These decisions supersede the previous white, animation-heavy site.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Creative thinking and practical technology belong in the same conversation. An orbital brand diagram and two readable service groups express that connection.

OWN-WORLD: Deep navy #101725, lime #d5f77c, cyan #8ce0e6, coral #f0a5a6 and paper #f5f5ef. Sora typography, fine tinted rules, generous section spacing, geometric illustrations and modest corner radii.

STORY: Visitors understand the offer immediately, explore eight verified services, meet the approach and people, see only verified client logos, and prepare an enquiry or use direct contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Oversized headline left, lime emphasis on its final line. Static orbital diagram right. Two actions directly under the copy. A quiet service summary anchors the viewport.

FORM: User-pinned direction, implemented directly. Responsive two-column hero and a single-page visitor path. Signature interaction: restrained arrow translation on actionable links. No hidden entrance states or continuous animation.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
